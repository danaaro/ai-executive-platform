    Executive Search

    The appointment, assessed against the organization it is joining.

    For CEOs, C-suite and critical leadership roles where getting it wrong is costly.

    • Start with the business: its ambition, reality and what this leader needs to deliver
    • Define what success requires, then build the profile around it
    • Search globally, beyond the obvious candidates
    • Look beyond credentials to evidence of what they can actually deliver
    • Make the case clear: why this person, where the risks are, and what gives us conviction
    • Carry what we learn forward once the leader starts
