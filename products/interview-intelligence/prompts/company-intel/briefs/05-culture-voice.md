# Brief 05 — Culture & Employee Voice
**Shelf life:** 365 days · **Output:** `research/05-culture-voice.md`

## Goal
Capture how the company describes its culture (**STATED**) and how employees experience
it (**LIVED**). Keep the two strictly separate. This module feeds the culture profile,
the most important output of the whole system.

## Questions to answer
### Stated culture
1. Official values, mission, and culture statements (exact names of values).
2. What the careers site emphasizes: benefits, flexibility, growth, impact, stability?
3. How leadership talks about culture and ways of working (CEO letters, talks).
4. What the company celebrates publicly on LinkedIn and its blog: awards, people
   stories, behaviors it holds up as examples.
5. Strategy statements that imply culture (e.g. "AI-first", "customer obsession").

### Lived culture (employee voice)
6. Glassdoor: overall rating, category ratings (culture & values, work-life balance,
   senior management, career opportunities, compensation), CEO approval,
   recommend-to-friend %, and 12-month trend if shown.
7. Recurring **pros** themes and **cons** themes, with rough frequency where the source
   provides it.
8. Differences by role family or location, if clearly visible (e.g. engineering vs sales).
9. Any Glassdoor exports or review files in `inputs/`: analyze these for themes too.

## Sources, in priority order
1. `inputs/` folder (handbooks, values decks, Glassdoor exports): highest trust
2. Careers page, values/culture page, CEO letters
3. Glassdoor public pages (overall ratings, category ratings, highlighted themes)
4. Company LinkedIn posts (public)
5. Press interviews with leaders about culture; "best places to work" lists

## Module-specific rules
- **IGNORE all demographic breakdowns.** Glassdoor shows ratings by race, gender,
  sexual orientation, disability, caregiver status, and similar. Never record them.
- Report **themes**, not single reviews. One dramatic review is not evidence.
- Record the date range of the reviews you're summarizing.
- Keep STATED and LIVED in separate sections. Don't reconcile them; that's the
  synthesizer's job.
