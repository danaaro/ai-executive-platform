# Brief 06 — Group & Portfolio
**Shelf life:** 180 days · **Output:** `research/06-group-structure.md`

## Goal
Map the group this company belongs to and the companies it owns, so we understand where
decisions are made and which sister or subsidiary businesses matter. Many hiring and
leadership decisions sit one level up (parent) or one level down (brands, business units).

## Questions to answer
1. **Is it part of a larger group?** Parent company or controlling owner (one level up only):
   name, what it is, ownership stake, and whether it actively manages the company (board
   seats, shared services, a holding structure). If the company is standalone, say so and
   stop here for this question.
2. **Major companies inside the group:** up to 8 subsidiaries, brands or business units
   that matter most (by size, revenue or strategic weight). For each:
   - what it does, and in which market/country
   - its role in the group (core business, growth bet, recently acquired, being sold...)
   - size if published (employees, revenue, users), with date
   - its top leader (CEO / managing director / GM): name, role, since when
   - its most significant change in the last 12 months (leadership, restructuring, M&A,
     launch), with date
3. **Recent portfolio moves:** acquisitions, divestments and mergers inside the group in the
   last 24 months, with dates.
4. **How the group is run:** is it centralized (shared platform, group functions) or
   federated (country/brand CEOs with their own P&L)? Use only stated or evidenced facts.

## Sources, in priority order
1. **Official company website: the source of truth.** `company-website-*.md` in `inputs/`
   (read by the platform today) plus the site's
   live pages: companies/brands, about, team. When it disagrees with
   any other source about the company itself, the website wins; record the conflict.
2. **Documents we uploaded** (other files in `inputs/`)
3. Each subsidiary's own website: about / leadership / press pages
4. Parent company's website, investor relations and annual report
5. Official press releases and reputable business and trade press
Sources from 3 on are found by web search: use them only after 1 and 2, and never over them.

## Module-specific rules
- **Leaders:** public professional information only (same rule as module 04). A leader
  named only by an org-chart or data aggregator is "unconfirmed (aggregator only, <date>)".
- **One level up, one level down.** Do not map the parent's entire portfolio. For a
  private-equity owner, record the owner and stake only.
- Keep each entity to a few lines. Summarize; don't paste.
