HOW WE ASSESS
Hiring is a prediction. We assess every candidate on the same four attributes, get two independent views on each, and decide on evidence.

1. PROVEN EXPERIENCE · Can they deliver what this role requires? (defined for each role)
Results like the ones this role needs; job knowledge and the scale they handled; what they did themselves, not their team.

2. {{company}} CONTEXT · Can they succeed in our reality? (defined for each role)
Delivered before in conditions like ours; our ownership, pace, organisation and markets; never "someone like us".

3. LEADERSHIP & FUTURE READINESS · Can they lead the business into the future? (SP&P framework)
Chosen from our framework: 17 competencies in four clusters (Lead the Future, the Business, People, Self); 5 core competencies for every executive, plus 2-3 the hiring manager picks for the role; rated on the framework's rubric, calibrated to N-1 or C-level.

4. MUTUAL FIT · Can we succeed together? (defined for each role)
The job they want vs the job there is; what they need from the manager; motivation and expectations.

THE PROCESS
1. Recruiter screen, 60 min: early signals, must-haves tested live.
2. Four interviews, 60 min each: each interviewer takes two attributes, so every attribute gets two views.
3. Case: watch them work, with one change of facts mid-way.
4. References: a former manager and a peer, checking results.
5. Decision: everyone scores alone within 24 h; the decision-maker speaks last.

FOUR PANEL RULES
• You choose who takes what. Agree before the first candidate which two attributes each interviewer covers.
• Cross-functional and mixed. Include people from the functions the role works with, a mix of genders, and nationalities where the company is international.
• Same questions for everyone. Pick your questions from the bank once, then ask them to every candidate.
• Judge evidence, not polish. Results, numbers and their own role. Never titles, schools, famous employers or accent.
