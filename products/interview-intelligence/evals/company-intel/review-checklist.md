# Review Checklist — before marking a profile reviewed

Use this on every culture profile (and company brief). All ✅ = mark reviewed.
Anything ❌ = send feedback, re-run, or fix manually.

## Accuracy
- [ ] **Spot-check 5 facts** in the research files: open the sources. Are they real,
      correctly stated, and dated?
- [ ] **Freshness:** are headcount, financials, and leadership current, not old snapshots?
- [ ] If you know this company: does the "lived culture" match reality as you know it?

## Usefulness
- [ ] **Competitor test:** would the "who thrives here" traits be WRONG for a direct
      competitor? If they'd fit any company, they're generic.
- [ ] **Surprise test:** did you learn at least one thing the careers page doesn't say?
- [ ] **Interview probes** are behavioral ("Tell me about a time...") and specific to
      this company.
- [ ] **Open questions** are worth asking a hiring manager.

## Honesty
- [ ] Confidence levels match the evidence (thin module → low confidence).
- [ ] "Not found" gaps are stated, not papered over.
- [ ] Stated vs lived gaps are named where evidence shows them.

## Fairness (hard stop if any fails)
- [ ] Every trait is an observable behavior, not a personality type.
- [ ] No demographic data or proxies (age, generation, gender, nationality, family
      status, "culture like ours", school prestige).
- [ ] Stakeholder info is public and professional only.
