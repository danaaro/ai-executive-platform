# Job Description Agent — Question Bank

> STATUS: 🟢 v3 (2026-10-29) — **Job Discovery Questionnaire**, Susan's final list (`../../../../input/Questions for the Hiring Manager _JD.md`, 11 sections / 68 questions, verbatim) plus the three questions and one probe agreed in D1 (PRD v3, 2026-10-17). Supersedes v2 (20 sections / 128 questions). This questionnaire is the workflow the JD agent executes — every question below must end the session with a coverage status.

**Coverage statuses:** `answered` · `inferred` (credited from something the HM said elsewhere, from their documents, or from the company context — confirm briefly when it matters) · `unknown` · `not_yet_decided` · `skipped` (HM explicitly declined).

**Parenthetical examples are interviewer guidance, never script.** Where a question carries a bracketed list — `(e.g., business growth, new initiative, restructuring, new capability)` — that list tells the interviewer what the question is reaching for. It is not part of the question. Never read it out, never offer it as options, and never re-ask a question with the examples attached. Ask the bare question; use the examples only to judge whether the answer addressed it.

**Section handling tags:** `[public]` may inform JD text directly · `[internal]` informs tone, screening and downstream agents but is never quoted in the JD · `[mixed]` per-question judgment (candor answers stay internal).

**Conversation rule (Dana, 2026-10-29):** ask ONE question per turn, or at most TWO when they are directly connected (one depends on the other, or they are two halves of the same thought). Never more. Credit answers given in passing rather than re-asking them, and let each answer shape the next question — but the coverage record at the end must still account for every question ID.

**Added questions (D1):** 4.8 (scope in numbers), 5.7 (Executive Committee) and 6.8 (why people struggled in similar roles, internal). **Probe on 2.1:** split the outcomes into what must be visible in the first 6–12 months and what follows in 12–24 months (the JD's "What Success Looks Like" needs both).

## 1. Business Context & Purpose  `[public]`

| # | Question |
|---|---|
| 1.1 | Please describe the role and its purpose in your own words. |
| 1.2 | Why does this role exist? |
| 1.3 | Is this a new position or a replacement? |
| 1.4 | If it is a replacement, what worked well in the previous setup or with the previous person? What did not? |
| 1.5 | If it is a new role, what prompted the need? (e.g., business growth, new initiative, restructuring, new capability) |
| 1.6 | What business problem is this role expected to solve? |
| 1.7 | Which business priorities, strategic initiatives, or OKRs does this role directly support? |

## 2. Objectives & Expected Outcomes  `[public]`

| # | Question |
|---|---|
| 2.1 | What are the three to five most important outcomes this person must achieve during their first 12–18 months? (probe: what must be visible in the first 6–12 months, and what follows in 12–24 months?) |
| 2.2 | Imagine this person has been in the role for one year and has significantly exceeded your expectations. What have they accomplished? |

| # | Question |
|---|---|

## 4. Decision Making & Scope  `[public]`

| # | Question |
|---|---|
| 4.1 | What level of authority will this role have? |
| 4.2 | Which decisions can this person make independently? |
| 4.3 | Which decisions require approval? |
| 4.4 | What resources, budget, or tools will this person control? |
| 4.5 | Will this role primarily execute existing processes or create new ones? |
| 4.6 | How much ambiguity should this person be comfortable operating in? |
| 4.7 | Is success more dependent on execution, strategic thinking, or both? |
| 4.8 | What is the scope of the role in numbers? (e.g., revenue or P&L, budget, headcount, countries or sites) |
