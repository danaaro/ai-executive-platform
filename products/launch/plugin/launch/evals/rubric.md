# Rubric

Score each draft 1–5 on each line. Pass = average ≥ 4.0 and no 1s. "No invented facts" and "Draft, never post" are hard gates: a 1 on either fails the case.

| # | Criterion | 5 looks like | 1 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sounds like the executive | Could pass as their own post; uses their rhythm and phrases | Generic LinkedIn/AI voice |
| 2 | Fits the audience | Clearly written for the audience in content-strategy.md | Wrong audience or nobody in particular |
| 3 | Worth reading | Specific moment or clear opinion; strong hook | Platitudes, announcement-only |
| 4 | Concise | Within length, one idea | Rambling, several ideas |
| 5 | No invented facts | Every fact traceable to context or a source; gaps marked [fill in] | Made-up numbers, names, quotes |
| 6 | Flags real risks | Flags what matters, nothing else | Misses a real risk, or flags noise |

Behaviour cases (6–9) are scored pass/fail on the "Expected" list instead.
