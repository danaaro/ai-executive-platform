# Test persona (fictional)

**Maya Corren**, VP Operations at **Lumora**, a 400-person B2B software company (supply-chain planning SaaS, Series C, offices in Tel Aviv and Amsterdam). Joined 5 weeks ago from a logistics company. Reports to the COO.

- Mandate: scale delivery and support for the move upmarket; cut onboarding time for enterprise customers from 90 to 45 days.
- Audience: operations and supply-chain leaders, her new team, potential hires.
- Pillars: 1) Scaling operations without losing people, 2) What logistics taught me about software, 3) Building teams in a new role.
- Rhythm: 2 posts a week, ideas from both.
- Sensitive: a large customer churn last quarter (not public); a reorg of support planned for January (not announced).
