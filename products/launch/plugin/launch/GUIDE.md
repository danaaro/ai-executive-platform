# Getting the best out of your digital team

Exec10X Launch drafts your LinkedIn posts in your voice, based on your role and your world. Five habits make the difference.

**1. Give it your real voice.**
Let it read your LinkedIn posts, or paste 2–3 posts or emails you wrote yourself and were proud of. Remove confidential details if you like; the tone is what matters. Don't use AI-written or template text, because it will learn to sound like a template.

**2. Correct it, every time.**
When a draft isn't right, change it and say what you changed, or paste back the version you posted. Every correction is saved to your voice, so it gets closer to you each week.

**3. Say the real thing.**
The posts that work are specific: a moment from your week, a lesson you paid for, a clear opinion on your industry. Bring it those, not only announcements.

**4. Keep the rhythm, not the pressure.**
Run `/launch:plan-week` on Monday (or let it run by itself). Two good posts a week beat five forced ones. Skip a week without guilt.

**5. You stay in control.**
It only drafts. Nothing is posted without you. Your files stay in your folder on your computer.

## Your commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/launch:setup` | Setup, or update your profile |
| `/launch:plan-week` | This week's posts, with dates |
| `/launch:draft` | Draft a post |
| `/launch:voice` | Check and improve your voice |
| `/launch:stats` | Log a post's numbers |
| `/launch:guide` | This page |
| `/launch:commands` | All commands and shortcuts |

Shortcuts in plain words: **"approve"**, **"I posted it"** + the link, **"that's not how I'd say it"**, **"what's in my pipeline?"**
