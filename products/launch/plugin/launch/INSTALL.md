# Installing Exec10X Launch

About 2 minutes to install, then about 5 minutes to set up.

## What you need
- The **Claude desktop app** on your Mac or PC, signed in.
- A **paid Claude plan** (Pro or higher).
- Optional: the **Claude in Chrome** extension, so Launch can read your LinkedIn posts and post numbers for you. Without it, you paste them.

## Install
1. Open the Claude desktop app.
2. In the left sidebar, click **Customize**, then the **Plugins** tab.
3. Click **Add**, then choose **upload a plugin file**, and pick the file `exec10x-launch.zip` we sent you.
4. Make sure **Exec10X Launch** is switched on.

## One setting to switch on
Go to **Settings › Capabilities** and turn on **Code execution and file creation**. Launch needs it to keep your Excel tracker.

## Set up
1. Start a new task (Cowork) in Claude.
2. Type `/launch:setup` and press Enter.
3. Answer a few short questions. Have your LinkedIn profile link and company website handy.

You'll finish with your first post drafted. Type `/launch:commands` anytime to see every command.

## After installing: the three golden rules
1. **Real voice:** the more of your real writing it sees, the more it sounds like you.
2. **Correct it:** change the draft and say what you changed. Every correction teaches it.
3. **You post:** it only drafts. You decide what goes out and when.

## Where your data lives
Everything is in one folder you choose (usually **Documents › My Exec Team**): your profile, voice, strategy, `My Commands.md` and `Content Tracker.xlsx`. Nothing is sent to Exec10X. Claude works with these files the same way it works with any files you share with it.

## Weekly plan on autopilot (optional)
When you first run `/launch:plan-week`, Launch offers to run it every Monday morning. It runs when your computer is on and Claude is open.

## Remove it
**Customize › Plugins**, open Exec10X Launch, and choose **Remove**. Your folder stays where it is; delete it yourself if you want.
