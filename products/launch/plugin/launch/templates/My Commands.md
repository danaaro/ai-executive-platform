# Your Exec10X Launch commands

Type `/` in Claude and start typing `launch` to see them all.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/launch:setup` | First-time setup (about 5 minutes). Run it again anytime to update your profile. |
| `/launch:plan-week` | Suggests this week's posts, with dates, and adds them to your pipeline. |
| `/launch:draft` | Drafts a post: from your pipeline, your own idea, or a link you paste. |
| `/launch:voice` | Shows how well it knows your voice, and lets you add samples or fix it. |
| `/launch:stats` | Logs the numbers for a post you published. |
| `/launch:guide` | How to get the best out of your digital team (one page). |
| `/launch:commands` | This list. |

**Or just say it in plain words:**

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| "approve" | The draft is marked Approved in your tracker. |
| "I posted it" + the link | It's marked Posted, with the final text saved. |
| "that's not how I'd say it" | It learns the difference and offers to save it to your voice. |
| "what's in my pipeline?" | A short list of ideas, drafts and due dates. |

It only drafts. Nothing is ever posted without you.
