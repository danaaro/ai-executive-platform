# Writing a LinkedIn post for an executive

## Default shape (voice.md overrides any of this)

- 120–250 words. One idea per post.
- First line is the hook: a specific moment, a clear opinion or a surprising fact. It must work on its own before "…see more".
- Grounded in something real from their role, company or week. Practitioner, not commentator.
- Short paragraphs, natural sentences. No stacked one-line fragments for drama.
- Ends with a clear point or strong statement. No engagement bait ("Thoughts?", "Agree?", "Comment YES").
- 3–5 relevant hashtags at the end, unless voice.md says otherwise.
- No buzzwords: leverage, synergy, game-changer, unlock, delve, landscape, journey (unless they really use them).

## Researched topics (light)

- Use web search only when the command allows it, and stay within its limit.
- Every researched fact comes with its link and date in the tracker's Sources column.
- Prefer the last 30 days. Prefer the user's industry, company news and their pillars.
- If you can't verify something, leave it out or mark it `[check]`.

## Risk flags (after the draft, max 2 lines)

Flag when a draft touches:
- unannounced company news, numbers, customers, deals or people moves,
- anything that could read as speaking for the company on a sensitive topic,
- named people who may not want to be named,
- legal, regulatory or market-sensitive topics (public company, M&A, layoffs),
- a claim that needs a source.

For a sensitive post, also give **one alternative angle** with a one-line label (for example "Safer: the lesson, without the numbers").

## Output format

1. Status line (see rules).
2. One line on the angle you took and why.
3. The draft in a plain code block, so it copies cleanly.
4. Flags, if any.
5. The learning-loop line.
6. Next step in one line: "Say 'approve' and I'll mark it Approved in your tracker."
7. Commands footer.
