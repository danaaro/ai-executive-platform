# Voice method

The whole product lives or dies on this: drafts must sound like the executive, not like AI.

## Sources of voice (user picks one)

1. **Their LinkedIn posts, read in Chrome.** If Claude in Chrome is available, open their profile's recent activity and read their last 5–10 own posts (not reposts or comments). Only read; never click like, comment or post.
2. **Pasted posts.** 2–3 posts they wrote themselves.
3. **Pasted emails or messages.** Real ones they wrote and liked. Post voice differs from email voice, so mark the profile `provisional` until there are real post samples or 3 learned corrections.

**Not acceptable:** text written by AI or an assistant, templates, or one-liners. If that's all they offer, say why in one sentence and ask once more. If they still can't, carry on and mark the profile `provisional`.

They may remove confidential names or numbers before pasting. Tone is what matters.

## What to extract into voice.md

Keep each line short and concrete, with an example phrase where possible:

- Tone (e.g. warm and direct, dry humour, formal)
- Typical length and structure (story first? point first? lists?)
- How they open (the hook pattern) and how they close
- Sentence rhythm (short and punchy, long and flowing)
- Signature words and phrases
- Words, styles and things they never use (emoji? hashtags? exclamation marks?)
- What they post about and what they never post about

Then store the samples verbatim, labelled with source and date.

## Learning loop (every draft)

1. End each draft with one line: "Change anything that doesn't sound like you, or paste your final version back."
2. When they edit, paste back the final text or say "not how I'd say it": compare it with your draft, name the difference in one line ("You cut the opening story and led with the point"), then ask once: "Save that to your voice?"
3. If yes, append it under **Learned preferences** in `voice.md` with the date. Update the counts at the top (drafts, corrections).
4. When they share the version they actually posted, offer to add it as a new sample.
5. After 3 saved corrections, or once real post samples exist, remove the `provisional` flag and say so.
