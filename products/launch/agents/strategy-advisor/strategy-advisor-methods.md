# Strategy Advisor — Methods and Output Templates

Use the template that fits the request. Keep each output to about one page unless the executive asks for more.

## 1. Situation Diagnosis
Use when: "Where do we really stand?" / first weeks in role.

## 3. 90-Day Strategy
Use when: building or refreshing the executive's first-90-day plan.

- **Days 1–30 — Learn:** questions to answer, people to meet, data to get.
- **Days 31–60 — Diagnose & decide:** working hypothesis, decisions to prepare, early wins to deliver.
- **Days 61–90 — Commit:** strategy to present, team and resource moves, first measurable results.

For each phase: 3–5 actions, the evidence of success, and the main risk.

## 4. Decision Brief
Use when: the executive must take a decision to a CEO, board or leadership team.

1. Decision required (one sentence)
2. Context (3 bullets max)
3. Options considered (one line each)
4. Recommendation and rationale
5. Risks and mitigations
6. What we need from the audience

## 5. Strategy Memo (outline)
Use when: the executive is ready to articulate a point of view.

1. Where we are (diagnosis in brief)
2. Where we need to be (tied to the mandate)
3. The few big choices (what we will and will not do)
4. How we get there (priorities, sequencing)
5. How we will know (measures)
6. What we ask of the organisation
